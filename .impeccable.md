## Design Context

### Users
Visitors discovering Hux, his writing, projects, and talks. First-time visitors need a brief introduction and an invitation to explore; returning visitors use the site as a personal OS with keyboard shortcuts and media surfaces.

### Brand Personality
Personal, curious, considered. Short first-person prose, informed by the supplied Shu Ding and Anthony Fu biography references.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retain the site's editorial prose and tactile system UI. The About surface is centered, brief, and floats over a blurred page. Its distinctive accent is a Siri-inspired shader border with flowing, colorful light along the screen edges. Respect both themes and the existing fonts and ink tokens.

### Design Principles
- Let the introduction breathe; avoid a boxed dialog in the middle.
- Concentrate expressive color and motion at the perimeter.
- Make projects tangible through small inline badges and existing media launchers.
- Preserve the visitor's place when opening and dismissing system surfaces.
- Support keyboard, touch, reduced motion, and readable small screens.
