## Design Context

### Users
Hux.Pro visitors exploring Hux's writing and work through a personal OS homepage. Ambient weather provides a sense of place and time without interrupting reading or navigation (docs/design-philosophy.md).

### Brand Personality
Calm, personal, tactile. Editorial content and native system surfaces coexist. Preserve the existing typography and navigation.

### Aesthetic Direction
For weather wallpaper, the creator explicitly requests the richness and quality of Apple's iOS automatic Weather Lock Screen: natural skies, dimensional clouds, continuous light and convincing motion. This request supersedes the older blanket prohibition on background gradients and decorative animation for this feature.

### Design Principles
- Weather should be recognizable through atmosphere, depth and light.
- Time and real meteorological data drive the scene; avoid arbitrary mood changes.
- Preserve readable foreground content across all weather and themes.
- Motion is slow and organic; respect reduced motion and stop rendering when hidden.
- Keep a graceful static fallback and bounded GPU cost.
