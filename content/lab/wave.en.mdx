The smallest interesting loop: eleven dots, one oscillation, and a delay that
scales with index. Stagger the start of each dot by a fraction of the period
and the row stops reading as *dots moving* and starts reading as *a wave
moving through dots*.

No state, no input — just time. It's the kind of thing that's worth keeping
around as a building block: drop it into a loader, a status indicator, or the
quiet corner of a card to make a surface feel like it's breathing.
