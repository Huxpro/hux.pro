A tiny study in making a surface feel **alive** under the cursor. The light is
nothing but a `radial-gradient` whose center is bound to normalized pointer
coordinates — no canvas, no WebGL, just one div re-rendering on `pointermove`.

The interesting part isn't the gradient; it's the restraint. The glow only
reaches full intensity while the pointer is inside, and fades back when it
leaves, so the card reads as *responsive* rather than *busy*.
