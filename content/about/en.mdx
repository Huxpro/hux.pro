# Hey, I'm Hux.

I <MagicLink post="dreamer">dream</MagicLink> of better *interfaces*: the ones we use, and the ones we use to build them.

I started making <MagicLink role="indie-flash">Flash</MagicLink> games at 10, earned my first few bucks on <MagicLink role="lxu-intern">motion graphics</MagicLink>, spent the 2010s crafting web and mobile apps for <Badge role="alitrip-engineer">Alibaba</Badge>, <Badge role="wepiao-lead">Wepiao</Badge>, and <Badge role="eleme-visiting">Ele.me</Badge>, then joined <Badge role="meta-engineer">Meta</Badge> to engineer compilers for the <Badge commit="reasonml">Web</Badge>, <Badge commit="xros-reality-labs">XR</Badge>, <Badge commit="hermes-engine" item={2}>React Native</Badge>, and <Badge commit="reactconf-2021-memo" icon="/badge-icons/react.dev.png">React</Badge>. I'm now at <Badge role="tech-lead-lynx">ByteDance</Badge> architecting <Badge commit="lynx-framework">Lynx</Badge>, a universal framework for building apps across platforms.

The kid rendering in Flash and the grad student<Fn n="1" /> proving in <MagicLink commit="wasmcert" item={3}>type theory</MagicLink> wanted the same thing: *interfaces*.

This site is an experiment in modeling myself as an OS<Fn n="2" />: the <MagicLink href="/works?type=talk">talks</MagicLink> I've given, the <MagicLink href="/writing">posts</MagicLink> I've written, the <MagicLink href="/works?type=project">projects</MagicLink> I've built, and even how I <MagicLink href="/prompt">prompt</MagicLink> myself.
