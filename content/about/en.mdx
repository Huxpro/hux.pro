# Hey, I'm Hux.

I build the layer between people and machines: languages, frameworks, and the interfaces on top of them. I've been doing it on the web for over a decade, with a detour through <Badge commit="wasmcert" item={3} icon="/img/works/wasmcert-poster.webp">Programming Languages Theory</Badge>.

Working on <Badge commit="lynx-framework" item={2}>Lynx</Badge> at ByteDance, the framework behind TikTok\
Previously <Badge commit="reactconf-2021-memo" icon="/badge-icons/react.dev.png">React Compiler</Badge> <Badge commit="hermes-engine" item={2}>Hermes</Badge> <Badge commit="reasonml">ReasonML</Badge> at Meta\
Before that <Badge commit="eleme-pwa" item={0}>Ele.me PWA</Badge> <Badge commit="alitrip-mobile-web">Alitrip</Badge> in China

I give [talks](/works?type=talk) and write [posts](/writing) about the web, React, and what app frameworks should become in the age of AI. Watch <Badge href="https://youtu.be/lGEMwh32soc">React without memo</Badge>, flip through <Badge href="https://huxpro.github.io/pwa-qcon2016/">PWA, 2016</Badge>, or play a round of <Badge app="lynx-flappy-bird" /> in a window. It all started with <Badge commit="hux-blog">Hux Blog</Badge> in 2015.

This site is an experiment in designing a personal website as an operating system. There is no navbar: press <Kbd>⌘K</Kbd> and ask it. Talks play on a stage, links open in an in-app browser, apps run in windows, and the wallpaper follows your sky. Everything here is a surface, and this is one of them: you can find it again in the command menu.
