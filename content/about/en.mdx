# Hey, I'm Hux.

I <MagicLink post="dreamer">dream</MagicLink> of better *interfaces*: the ones we use, and the ones we use to build them.

I started making <MagicLink commit="indie-flash" item={0}>Flash</MagicLink> games <MagicLink commit="indie-flash" item={1}>at 10</MagicLink>, earned my first few bucks on <MagicLink commit="lxu-intern" item={0}>motion graphics</MagicLink>, spent the 2010s crafting web and mobile apps for <Badge commit="alitrip-mobile-web">Alibaba</Badge>, <Badge commit="yupiaoer">Wepiao</Badge>, and <Badge commit="eleme-pwa">Ele.me</Badge>, then joined <Badge role="meta-engineer">Meta</Badge> to engineer compilers for the <Badge commit="reasonml">Web</Badge>, <Badge commit="xros-reality-labs">XR</Badge>, <Badge commit="hermes-engine">React Native</Badge>, and <Badge commit="reactconf-2021-memo" icon="/badge-icons/react.dev.png">React</Badge>. I'm now at <Badge role="tech-lead-lynx">ByteDance</Badge> architecting <Badge commit="lynx-framework">Lynx</Badge>, a universal framework for building apps across platforms.

This site is an experiment in modeling myself as an OS<Fn n="1" />: the <MagicLink href="/works?type=talk">talks</MagicLink> I've given, the <MagicLink href="/writing">posts</MagicLink> I've written, the <MagicLink href="/works?type=project">projects</MagicLink> I've built, and even how I <MagicLink href="/prompt">prompt</MagicLink> myself.
