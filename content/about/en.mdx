# Hey, I'm Hux.

I <Ref href="/writing/dreamer/en">dream</Ref> of making the *interfaces* between humans and computers better: the ones we use, and the ones we use to build them.

I started making <Ref commit="indie-flash">Flash</Ref> games at 10, earned my first few bucks on <Ref commit="lxu-intern">motion graphics</Ref>, spent the 2010s crafting web and mobile apps for <Badge commit="alitrip-mobile-web">Alibaba</Badge>, <Badge commit="yupiaoer">Wepiao</Badge>, and <Badge commit="eleme-pwa" item={0}>Ele.me</Badge>, then joined <Badge href="https://www.meta.com">Meta</Badge> to engineer compilers for the <Badge commit="reasonml">Web</Badge>, <Badge commit="xros-reality-labs">XR</Badge>, <Badge commit="hermes-engine" item={2}>React Native</Badge>, and <Badge commit="reactconf-2021-memo" icon="/badge-icons/react.dev.png">React</Badge>. I'm currently at <Badge href="https://www.bytedance.com">ByteDance</Badge>, architecting <Badge commit="lynx-framework" item={2}>Lynx</Badge>, a new universal *interface* for building apps across platforms.

It turns out the kid animating in Flash and the grad student<Fn n="1" /> staring at <Ref commit="wasmcert" item={3}>type theory</Ref> wanted the same thing all along: *interfaces*.

This site is an experiment in modeling a public *interface* to my mind, with a personal-OS aesthetic<Fn n="2" />: the <Ref href="/works?type=talk">talks</Ref> I've given, the <Ref href="/writing">posts</Ref> I've written, the <Ref href="/works?type=project">projects</Ref> I've built, and even how I <Ref href="/prompt">prompt</Ref> myself.
