The nuance encoded here

Meta and ByteDance roles act as context for many work artifacts — so they earn a place in the sticky chip. The PWA era's roles (Wepiao TLM, Alibaba FE) are themselves the artifacts — so the chip stays a single era, no role substrate. Eleme's role and "Upgrading Eleme" collapse into the same commit.
